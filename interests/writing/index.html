    Sometimes, I write things

    When I was in school, writing was a great hobby for me. I wrote essays and stories - even got them published in the school magazine a few times!

    Like many things, the competitive examination landscape of our country strips people of their hobbies. I do not know why so many people think this is a necessary sacrifice. Regardless, university has a way of breathing back life into these things if you know what you are doing.

    I have decided to write again! Whenever I find the time for it, that is. No poetry, though. I might be the only person alive with textual tone-deafness.
